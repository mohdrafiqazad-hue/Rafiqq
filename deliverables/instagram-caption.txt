Bail kaise milti hai? Step-by-step samjhiye ⚖️ (BNSS 2023)

1️⃣ Offence pehchaniye – Bailable (Sec 478) ya Non-bailable (Sec 480 / 483)
2️⃣ Arrest par adhikaar – Arrest ka kaaran batana zaroori (Sec 47), 24 ghante me Magistrate ke saamne peshi (Sec 58)
3️⃣ Regular Bail – Magistrate Court (Sec 480) ya Sessions / High Court (Sec 483)
4️⃣ Anticipatory Bail – Arrest ki aashanka par (Sec 482)
5️⃣ Default Bail – Chargesheet time par nahi: 90 din (gambhir apraadh) / 60 din (baaki) – Sec 187(3)
6️⃣ Bond & sharten – Har tareekh par haazri; shart todi to bail cancel ho sakti hai
